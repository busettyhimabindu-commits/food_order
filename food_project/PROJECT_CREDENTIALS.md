## 🛠️ Admin Panel Access Capabilities
Once logged in as Super Admin, you can:
1. **Manage Food Items (`/admin/foods`):** Add, edit, delete dishes, toggle availability, change prices, filter by restaurant/category/veg, and upload dish photos to Cloudinary.
2. **Manage Partner Restaurants (`/admin/restaurants`):** Add new dining spots, edit details, search, and upload restaurant storefront photos.
3. **Live Order Management (`/admin/orders`):** Track live customer orders, update delivery status, and view driver ETAs.
4. **User & Coupon Controls (`/admin/users`, `/admin/coupons`):** View user accounts and create promo discount codes.
